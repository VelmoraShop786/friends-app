What can I write to you about?

  • Help or a problem: tell us what happened and on which screen. A screenshot helps.
  • Report someone: the fastest way is inside the app: open the person’s profile and tap Report.
  • Suspended account: use the appeal form on the suspended-account page.
  • Delete my account: write from the email you registered with and use the subject “Delete my account”.
  • Privacy questions: ask about your data, or ask for a copy or a correction.

Please include

  • Your app ID number (shown on your profile).
  • A short description of the problem.

Never send your password or login codes to anyone. We will never ask for them.
