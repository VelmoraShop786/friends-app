Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information Friends App (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why we collect it, who can see it, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address (or your phone number if you sign up with one), your password (it is stored in a protected, scrambled form by our login provider, so we cannot read it), your name, gender, birthday (day and month only, we do not ask for the year), country, a short bio, and your profile picture. The app also gives your account a unique ID number.

Things you create. Messages you send in rooms and in private chats, pictures you upload (such as a profile picture or a room picture), friend requests, and reports or appeals you send us (including any picture you attach).

Voice. When you sit on a seat in a room, your voice is sent live to the other people in that room through our voice provider. We do not record or store room voice.

Activity in the app. Your friends and blocked people, the rooms you own or enter, your seat, your XP, level, profile frames and other in-app items, your notification and privacy settings, and whether you are online.

Safety records. Reports about you or made by you, warnings, suspensions and appeals, so that we can keep the community safe.

Device and technical information. The type of browser and phone you use (for example “Android · Chrome”) and a random code that lets us keep your account logged in on one device at a time. Our hosting and service providers also process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the service. The app stores some data on your own device (your login session, a saved copy of recent profile and room information, and your choices) so it opens faster.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account, show your profile, deliver messages and voice, and let you add friends.
  • To send you login codes by email and messages from “System” inside the app (for example about your XP, frames, or the result of a report).
  • To keep the app safe: handle reports, block abuse and spam, stop cheating, and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
  • To give you levels, XP and rewards, and to keep your settings.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information. The app does not currently show advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other users can see your name, ID number, profile picture, bio, level and frame. Your country flag, gender and birthday are shown too, unless you hide them in Settings > Privacy.
  • People in a room can see the messages you write there and that you are in the room or on a seat.
  • Private messages are visible only to you and the person you are chatting with.
  • Our team can see reports and the related messages or pictures when we review them.

4. Service providers we use

We share information with companies that help us run the app, only as needed for that purpose:

  • Supabase: database, login and file storage (our data is held in the Asia-Pacific region).
  • LiveKit: live voice in rooms.
  • Netlify: hosting of the website and app files.
  • Google: sending login emails through Gmail, and fonts that the pages load from Google Fonts (which receive your IP address when they load).
  • jsDelivr: delivery of open-source code libraries used by the app.

If we introduce purchases, they will be handled by Google Play, and we will not receive your card details. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

5. Where your data is stored and how it is protected

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is protected by access rules, so one user cannot read another user’s private data. Our providers may process data in other countries. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time: send an email to velmoraofficial786@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my account”. After we confirm it is you, we delete your profile, pictures and the messages you sent, normally within 30 days. We may keep a limited record that is needed to protect other people and enforce our rules (for example reports and suspensions), and records we must keep by law.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your name, bio, gender, birthday, country and picture from your profile.
  • Hide your birthday, country or gender, and choose who can send you friend requests, in Settings > Privacy.
  • Choose which red numbers and notifications you see in Settings > Notifications.
  • Block or report any person from their profile.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by email.

8. Age limit

The app is only for people who are 18 years old or older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, please email us and we will review it and remove the account if needed.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the date at the top. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email velmoraofficial786@gmail.com or visit our Contact Us page.
