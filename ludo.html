Ludo

Quick match

Practice (free, no coins)

Play against computer players to learn the game. No coins are won or lost.
Rules: you need a 6 to bring a token out. A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another throw. Every number you throw is kept: use the numbers in any order, on any token, or throw again while you still have a throw. Three 6s in a row cancel the numbers you have not used. Stars and start squares are safe. You have 12 seconds for each action.
